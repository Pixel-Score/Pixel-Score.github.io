      How to use SHS Story Map

      Each episode is drawn as a map: cards hold who speaks and what they say, and arrows show where the story goes next, including choices, minigame wins and losses, and variable checks.

      1. Pick an episode

      • Click the episode box at the top to see every episode, grouped by game and season.
      • Type to narrow the list: part of a title (prom), a game (cod), or numbers (s2 4 = season 2, episode 4; e10).
      • Use the game box to show only Surviving High School or Cause of Death.
      • Have your own decoded file? Use Open JSON or drag the .json onto the page.

      2. Choose a view

      • Scene map: one card per scene. Click a scene to open it.
      • Scene flow: every step of one scene. Pick the scene from the box next to the view buttons or from the side panel.
      • Everything: the whole episode in one picture.
      • Dashed boxes at the edge of a scene lead to other scenes. Click one to follow it.

      3. Read the map

      • Click a card to open its details: the full script, where it leads, what leads to it, and the raw JSON.
      • Click an arrow's label to jump to where it goes.
      • Arrow colours show the kind of link: choices, win, lose, checks, scene jumps. Open Key (bottom left) for the full list.
      • The side panel has Scenes, Variables (where each one is set and checked) and Search (every line of dialogue).

      Options

      • Cues: also show backgrounds, music and sound effects.
      • Routines: show or hide scenes kept as exact game code (blue-green cards).
      • Lines per node: how much dialogue each card shows before "more".
      • ◐ switches light and dark. The address bar always points at what you're looking at, so you can share it.
